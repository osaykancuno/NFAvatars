NOT FUNGIBLE MOMENTS

Somebody at the party pulled the rug. You have their photo on your phone. Everybody looks alike. Nobody is fungible. Run.

How to play

  • Tap left or right to change lane, or tap the person you want.
  • Run into the person on the photo: caught.
  • Bump anybody else and your phone loses a bar. Five bars, then it's over.
  • Keys: left and right arrows, or 1 2 3 for a lane. P pauses, M mutes.

This trial lasts 60 seconds and mints nothing. All six levels, the one who did it and the mint are on THE WALL ARCADE.

FROM THE TRIAL TO YOUR NFT

How to mint a Not Fungible Avatar.

1 credit = 1 run = 1 Not Fungible Avatar

There is no open mint and no allowlist: every Not Fungible Avatar is earned in a real run on THE WALL ARCADE, scored by SuperRare's server, and minted when that run ends.

  1. Warm up herePlay the free trial above as many times as you like. Same people, same places, same rules: only shorter.
  2. Go to THE WALL ARCADEOpen THE WALL ARCADE and pick Not Fungible Moments.
  3. Sign in with SuperRareConnect with your wallet through SuperRare Connect. If you have no credits yet, buy a pack of THE WALL ARCADE credits: one balance works for every game of the arcade.
  4. Spend 1 credit, play up to 3 minutesThe game asks you to confirm before it spends the credit. Your run is played and scored on SuperRare's server, so nobody can fake a score.
  5. Meet your personWhen the run ends you see who pulled the rug: their role, look, moment, expression, item and tier. Catch them on level 6 for Legendary.
  6. Mint itPress Mint on the end screen: SuperRare opens the checkout, you confirm in your wallet and your Not Fungible Avatar lands there, on Ethereum. Each run mints once, and never the same person twice: 10,000 at most, all different.
  7. Show it offYour Not Fungible Avatar is an animated GIF on OpenSea, living its moment on loop. Share it with #NotFungibleAvatars.

THE IDEA

A tribute to the NFC Lisbon community.

Every year NFC Lisbon fills the city with the people who make this space what it is: artists and collectors, builders and founders, curators, DJs, the crew who keeps it running and the friends you meet at brunch. What stays with you is never a floor price. It is the moments: the selfie with someone you only knew by their profile picture, the toast, the standing ovation, the first mint on a phone, the high five.

Not Fungible Moments is a small game about telling people apart, and Not Fungible Avatars is the collection they leave behind. Every person is drawn once and only once, because in this community nobody is fungible.

It is part of THE WALL ARCADE, a remix of THE WALL experiment at NFC Summit 2027 in Lisbon, made with love by @osaykancuno.
