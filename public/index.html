FAQ

Questions, anon?

How much does it cost?

The trial on this site is free and mints nothing. On THE WALL ARCADE one full run costs 1 SuperRare credit, and that run's person can be minted when it ends: 1 credit = 1 run = 1 Not Fungible Avatar (plus network gas for the mint).

Which chain? Where is the contract?

Ethereum, minted through SuperRare. The contract address appears here at launch.

How many Not Fungible Avatars will exist?

10,000 at most, and never the same person twice. One per paid run that gets minted: each run can mint only once, so the supply grows with the players until it hits the cap.

What do I get?

An animated Not Fungible Avatar: the person you caught, living their moment on loop, with a voice of its own.

Is my avatar a real agent?

The pieces are standard and open: an ERC-8004 identity, an ERC-6551 wallet, a voice from its traits and a job it does on its own, the Daily. Registering the identity and creating the wallet happen once it is minted, and they are the owner's choice. Today the voice is written rules, not an AI model.

How do I get a Legendary?

Clear all six levels and catch the one who pulled the rug, on the rooftop, before your phone dies. No luck, no allowlist, just your eyes.

Is it fair?

On THE WALL ARCADE every run is scored by SuperRare's server, not by your browser. Every trait comes from that verified run: one mint per run, no trait above your tier, never the same person twice.

Who are the characters?

Original pixel people: artists, collectors, devs, founders, curators, streamers, degens, DJs, whales, bodyguards, baristas, gamers, meme lords, photographers, skaters and chefs. Every look, every background, independent of the role.
