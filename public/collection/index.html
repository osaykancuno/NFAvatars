HOW THE COLLECTION WORKS

Nobody is fungible.

1 credit = 1 run = 1 Not Fungible Avatar

Earned, not bought

No public mint and no allowlist. Each Not Fungible Avatar comes from one paid run on THE WALL ARCADE, scored by SuperRare's server and minted when the run ends.

10,000 different people

16 roles, every skin, hair, hair color, facial hair and eyewear. 10,000 at most, on Ethereum, each person minted once: no two NFTs are the same person.

Traits from how you played

Moment, Expression and Item come from how you read people, the Background from your tier. Legendary only if you catch the one who did it.

Animated on OpenSea

Every token is a GIF of its person living their moment on loop, with the traits and a fallback image on-chain. The metadata can be locked forever.

Twelve traits

Background · Role · Pass · Hair · Hair Color · Facial Hair · Eyewear · Expression · Item · Moment · Tier · Score.
